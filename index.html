About Physio Boss (ExMed Hub)

We Are The Best For Physiotherapy & Rehab In Coimbatore

At Physio Boss (ExMed Hub - Pain & Rehabilitation Clinic), located at New Siddhapudhur, Coimbatore, we provide expert physiotherapy services tailored to your unique needs. From acute pain management to complex post-surgical and neurological rehabilitation, our goal is to help you regain strength, flexibility, and confidence in every movement.

We combine hands-on manual therapy, spinal mobilization, dry needling/cupping, kinesiology taping, and active therapeutic exercises with compassionate 1-on-1 patient education.
